# Approved resume bullets (LaTeX form)

Copy to `data/resume-variants.md`. Populate from your own supplied masters
before tailoring. This starter contains no approved bullets or skills.

For each role or project, add a `##` heading, a stable `[GROUP-ID]` line, and
each approved bullet on its own `- ` line. Copy the text inside `\resumeItem{}`
exactly, including LaTeX escaping. Alternate wordings of one fact share a group.
If a bullet also states another group's fact, put `  covers: GROUP-ID` on the
next line. Use at most one bullet for every stated group in a tailored resume.

Headings, dates, locations and project tech stacks come from the masters.
Review group assignments, skills and the unchanged master PDF before starting.
Never copy another person's sample achievements into this file.

Populate the Skills allowlist section with exact skill names from your masters,
separated by commas. Put personal exclusions in Banned phrases as `- phrase`
lines, or leave that section empty. Both sections start empty below.
